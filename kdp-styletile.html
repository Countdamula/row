Three directions for the KDP dashboard

Each panel renders the same five components with the same content — hero, Command Center plate with its weekly progress bar, a signature element, a chapter with its four indexes, and an AI prompt block. Only the design changes.

These are not three colourways. Each direction has its own structural idea, and that idea is the third block in every panel. Pick the one you would want to look at for five weeks straight.

A

The Bindery

The book-manufacturing floor, not the library. Bookcloth ground, palladium foil, and magenta proof ink reserved strictly for what is in revision. Structural idea: 40 chapters are five sewn gatherings of eight — progress is something physically bound, not a percentage.

The Ledger

Publishing as a going concern — the KDP half taken seriously. Pale ledger stock, red column rules, typewriter entries. Structural idea: the interface is ruled paper, statuses are rubber stamps, and every book is an account you read down a column.

C

Recto / Verso

The two-page spread as the entire interface, with a real gutter down the middle. Structural idea: reference lives on the verso, the work lives on the recto, and your notes live in the actual margin beside the line that provoked them — which is where the generatable notes go on every page.
